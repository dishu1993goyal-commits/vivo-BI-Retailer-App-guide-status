Privacy Policy

vivo BI Dashboard and vivo BI States apps · Last updated 22 October 2026

The vivo BI Dashboard and vivo BI States apps ("the apps") are internal business tools of the vivo Business Innovation (BI) team. They show the BI KPI Dashboard to authorised vivo staff and partners.

Information we collect

To confirm who is signing in, the apps verify your mobile number through Truecaller. Depending on how you verify, Truecaller may share with the app your mobile number and the name on your Truecaller profile. If you verify by missed call or OTP, you enter your name and mobile number yourself.

The apps do not collect your contacts, location, photos or messages. Call permissions, if you allow them, are used only by Truecaller to detect the verification missed call automatically.

How we use it

Your verification is used only to allow access to the apps. A record that your number was verified is kept on your own phone. We do not sell or share your information with anyone for advertising.

Third-party services

Mobile verification is provided by Truecaller (True Software Scandinavia AB) under its own privacy policy. The dashboard is hosted on Netlify.

Your choices

You can stop using the apps at any time by uninstalling them, which also removes the verification record from your phone.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the vivo Business Innovation team.
